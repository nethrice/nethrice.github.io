About the server

Not your typical SMP

nSMP started with one simple idea: a real vanilla-style survival server shouldn't need to charge players to stay online. No rank shops, no cosmetics locked behind a paywall, no "supporter" perks that give anyone an edge in the game. Every advantage on nSMP is earned by playing - never bought.

The whole server runs on high-end hardware and is paid for entirely by the owner. That means fewer decisions made to squeeze money out of players, and more decisions made because they're good for the world and the community living in it.

Whether you're a first-time survivalist or you've been playing since alpha, brand new to the server or a returning veteran - there's a place for you here. Everyone is welcome.

Gameplay

Vanilla at heart, with the essentials built in

nSMP keeps the core Minecraft experience intact and layers in the quality-of-life commands every good SMP needs - nothing more, nothing that breaks the game.

➹

Random Teleport

/rtp

Drop yourself into fresh, unclaimed terrain any time you need a new spot to build, mine, or just get away from spawn.

⇄

Teleport Requests

/tpa

Send a request to any player online and meet up instantly the moment they accept. No more running across the map.

⚒

Server Auction House

/auction

List items server-wide and let other players bid on them. A fair, public marketplace - no sketchy third-party trades.

⛁

Player & Server Shop

/shop

Buy and sell server-stocked items at fair, regulated prices whenever you like - all backed by in-game currency you earn.

🌱

Vanilla-First World

survival

No custom items, no gimmicky plugins changing how the game plays - just Minecraft, with the rough multiplayer edges smoothed out.

⛨

Fair by Design

no p2w

Every command and feature above is available to every player equally. Nothing on this list is ever sold for real money.

Player Economy

An in-game economy, without the pay-to-win

nSMP's economy runs entirely on currency and items earned through gameplay - never on real money. Here's how the two systems work together.

/shop

Server Shop

A serverside shop stocked with core items at fixed, regulated prices, so the market never gets out of hand.

  • Buy and sell serverside items any time, no auction needed
  • Prices are managed to stay fair for new and veteran players alike
  • Great for quick restocks between building or mining sessions
/auction

Auction House

A server-wide auction system for the items your shop run doesn't cover - rare drops, stacked loot, or anything worth more to the right buyer.

  • List an item and let players bid what it's actually worth to them
  • Everything is visible to the whole server - fully transparent
  • Currency earned in-game only - nothing here is bought with real money
